A tribute to cryptographer Len Sassaman is embedded in the Bitcoin blockchain at block 138,725 using an ASCII art portrait encoded in a transaction. Sassaman, a contributor to PGP and remailer technology, passed away on July 3rd. His work laid the groundwork for the privacy technologies that Bitcoin builds upon.
