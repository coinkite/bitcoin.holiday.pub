Bitcoin crashes from $7,900 to $3,800 in a single day as COVID-19 panic triggers a global liquidity crisis. Everything sold — stocks, bonds, gold, and Bitcoin. The crash would prove to be the bottom, with Bitcoin reaching $60,000 within a year.
