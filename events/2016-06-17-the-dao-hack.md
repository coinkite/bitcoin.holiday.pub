An attacker drains 3.6 million ETH (~$50M) from "The DAO" smart contract on Ethereum. The resulting hard fork to reverse the theft splits Ethereum into ETH and ETC — proving that "immutable" blockchains aren't when humans decide otherwise. Bitcoin's refusal to ever roll back transactions looks increasingly wise.
