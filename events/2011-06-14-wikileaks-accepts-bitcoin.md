title: WikiLeaks Accepts Bitcoin

isBitcoinEvent: true
---

WikiLeaks begins accepting Bitcoin donations after being cut off from traditional payment processors including Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal. One of Bitcoin's first real-world demonstrations of censorship resistance. Satoshi had earlier discouraged WikiLeaks from using Bitcoin, calling it "the hornet's nest."
