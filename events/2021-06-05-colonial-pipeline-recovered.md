The US Department of Justice recovers $2.3 million in Bitcoin paid as ransom to the DarkSide hacking group in the Colonial Pipeline attack. Initial media claims that "Bitcoin was hacked" were wrong — the FBI obtained the private key from a US-based server, not by breaking Bitcoin's cryptography.
