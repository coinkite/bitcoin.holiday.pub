Taproot activates at block 709,632 — Bitcoin's biggest protocol upgrade since SegWit. Introduces Schnorr signatures, enabling more efficient multisig transactions, improved privacy, and the foundation for more advanced smart contracts on Bitcoin. Achieved through Speedy Trial activation.
