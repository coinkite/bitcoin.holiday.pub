The FBI shuts down Silk Road and arrests Ross Ulbricht, seizing approximately 26,000 BTC. The marketplace had demonstrated both Bitcoin's utility for censorship-resistant commerce and its challenges. Bitcoin's price briefly dips, then rallies — proving the network is bigger than any single use case.
