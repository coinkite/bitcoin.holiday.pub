Bitcoin Cash (BCH) forks from Bitcoin at block 478,558, increasing the block size limit to 8 MB. The culmination of the blocksize war. Bitcoin keeps its 1 MB base blocks with SegWit, and the market eventually renders its verdict: BTC wins.
